Privacy

Last updated November 18, 2026

What we collect

When you sign up for the Friday letter, we collect your first name (optional), your email address, and, if you choose, whether you are a caregiver, living with a diagnosis, or a care professional. We use this only to send you the letter and to choose helpful topics.

What we never do

We never sell or rent your email address. We don't put advertising trackers on this site. We don't ask for health details about you or your family on this site.

Who handles your email

Our letters are sent through an email service provider that stores your address on our behalf. Every letter has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from future letters right away.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by replying to any Friday letter.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and mention it in the Friday letter.
