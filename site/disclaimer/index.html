Disclaimer

Last updated November 18, 2026

We are not medical professionals. Alzheimer's Lantern is written by people who care about families living with Alzheimer's, not by doctors, nurses, or other licensed health professionals.

Information only, not medical advice

Everything on this site, in the Friday letter, and in our printables, courses, and member tools is general information to help you learn and plan. It is not medical, legal, or financial advice, and it is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan.

Always talk to your doctor

Every person with Alzheimer's is different. Talk to a doctor or other qualified professional who knows you or your loved one before you make decisions about diagnosis, medicines, treatment, or care. Never ignore professional advice or delay getting it because of something you read here.

In an emergency

If someone is lost, hurt, or in danger, call 911 right away. If you're overwhelmed or in emotional crisis, call or text 988. For round-the-clock dementia support, call the Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline at 1-800-272-3900.

Accuracy

We work to keep our guides accurate and up to date, and we list our sources. Research and treatments change, though, and we can't promise that everything is complete or current. Using this site does not create a doctor-patient or any other professional relationship.

Other websites, products, and experts

Links to other websites are for convenience; we don't control their content. Mentioning a product, service, or treatment is not an endorsement. Guest experts in member Q&A sessions share general information and do not give advice about any individual's situation.

Not affiliated

Alzheimer's Lantern is an independent site. It is not affiliated with the Alzheimer's Association or any other organization.
