About Alzheimer's Lantern

A lantern is something you carry for someone else on a dark road. That's what we want Alzheimer's Lantern to be for families: a steady light, close at hand, that helps you see the next step.

Why we started

We started Alzheimer's Lantern because families facing Alzheimer's deserve clear, kind, practical help in one place, without jargon, clutter, or a sales pitch. Our founder's full story is coming soon.

How we write

  • Plain language, large print, and no jargon without an explanation.
  • Every guide lists its sources and the date it was last reviewed.
  • Health facts come from trusted sources like the National Institutes of Health and the Alzheimer's Association.
  • We don't promote supplements or anything claiming to cure or reverse Alzheimer's.
  • Any sponsor or affiliate link is clearly labeled.

Who checks our guides

We are not medical professionals, so we base every guide on trusted medical sources and list them at the end. We plan to add a qualified health professional to review our guides, and we'll introduce them here when we do.

Important note

We are not medical professionals. This site shares general information to help you understand Alzheimer's and caregiving. It is not medical advice and doesn't replace a doctor who knows you or your loved one. Always talk to a doctor about diagnosis, medicines, and treatment.
