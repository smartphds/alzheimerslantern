Everything here is free to read. Guides are written in plain language from trusted sources like the National Institutes of Health and the Alzheimer's Association, and each one lists its sources.

We are not medical professionals. These guides are general information, not medical advice. Always talk to your doctor about your own situation. In an emergency, call 911. Read our full disclaimer.

Need help right now?

  • Alzheimer's Association 24/7 Helpline: 1-800-272-3900
  • Eldercare Locator, to find local services: 1-800-677-1116
  • If you're overwhelmed or in emotional crisis, call or text 988
  • If someone is lost, hurt, or in danger, call 911
