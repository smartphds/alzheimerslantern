## Make choices simpler

- Offer two outfits, not a full closet.
- Lay clothes out in the order they go on.
- Put away out-of-season clothes and things they no longer wear.

## Choose easy clothes

- Pull-on pants with elastic waists
- Tops that open in the front, or that are loose enough to pull over
- Velcro instead of buttons and laces
- Slip-on shoes with non-skid soles
- Several sets of a favorite outfit, if they want to wear the same thing every day

## Give step-by-step help

- Give one short instruction at a time: "Put your arm in here."
- Hand them one item at a time.
- Let them do as much as they can, even if it takes longer or isn't perfect.
- Keep the room warm and private.

## Grooming

- Keep their usual routine: same products, same order.
- Use an electric razor, which is safer.
- Do hair care, nails, and makeup together if they enjoy it. Familiar scents and a nice look can lift their mood.
- Visit the dentist regularly and help with brushing teeth each day.

## If they refuse

Don't argue. Try again later, or have someone else help. Ask yourself whether it really matters today. Mismatched clothes are fine if they're clean, safe, and warm enough.
