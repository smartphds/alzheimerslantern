## Check for a medical cause first

Call the doctor if accidents start suddenly. A urinary tract infection (UTI), constipation, some medicines, and prostate problems can all cause accidents. A UTI can also cause sudden extra confusion. It is easy to treat once it's found.

## Why accidents happen

- They may not notice the urge in time.
- They can't find the bathroom, or don't recognize the toilet.
- Clothes are hard to undo quickly.
- They can't move fast enough.

## Things that help

- **Set a schedule.** Take them to the bathroom every 2 to 3 hours, and right after meals and before bed.
- **Watch for signs.** Restlessness, pulling at clothes, or pacing can mean they need to go.
- **Make the bathroom easy to find.** Leave the door open, use a sign or picture on the door, and keep a night light on.
- **Use easy clothes.** Elastic waists instead of buttons and zippers.
- **Keep drinks up during the day** and cut back a little in the two hours before bed. Don't limit fluids overall, since that can cause dehydration and UTIs.
- **Use products** like washable pads, adult briefs, and waterproof mattress covers.

## When an accident happens

Stay calm and kind. Say something simple like "Let's get you into something dry." Don't scold. Clean the skin gently and keep it dry to prevent sores.
